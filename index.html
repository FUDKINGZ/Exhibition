HOW TO PLAYTOUCH + KEYBOARD
Touch: L and R walk. LP and RP punch. LK and RK kick. BK holds block. TH throws only after the power meter is full.
Keyboard, home row: A or Left Arrow walks left. D or Right Arrow walks right. Hold to keep walking. S is LP. F is LK. J holds block. K is RP. L is RK. Semicolon is throw, and only after the meter is full.
A landed punch or kick builds power. Alternating sides, or punch and kick, builds a combo. A full meter arms 3 throws. A landed hit drops money. A block heals $1 and stops the hit. A whiff is a miss.
