GUIDE & DISCLAIMER
HOW TO PLAYTOUCH + KEYBOARD
Touch: L and R walk. LP and RP punch. LK and RK kick. BK holds block. TH throws only after the power meter is full.
Keyboard: S or the Left Arrow walks left. L or the Right Arrow walks right. Hold either key to keep walking. D is LK. F is LP. G holds block. H throws, and only after the meter is full. J is RP. K is RK.
A landed punch or kick builds power. Alternating sides, or a punch and a kick, builds a combo. A full meter arms 3 throws. A landed hit drops money. A block stops the hit and breaks the combo. It does not heal. A whiff is a miss. A throw only fires while throw ammo remains, and a blocked throw does no damage.
KEYBOARDASDF JKL;
S walks left. D is left kick. F is left punch. G holds block.
H throws. J is right punch. K is right kick. L walks right.
The Left Arrow and the Right Arrow also walk. Attack keys fire once per press.
MODESARCADE / EXHIBITION / 1 ON 1
Exhibition is free play. Every fighter and every arena is unlocked. It does not write fights, wins, losses, points, or XP. Pick the CPU level and the rounds on the matchup screen. Rounds are first to 2 in a 3-round match, or first to 3 in a 4-round match. Arcade is first to 2 in a 3-round match, and only Arcade writes the career book. The time limit is 2:00. A tie goes to sudden death. Power and throw ammo carry between rounds of the same match. Soundtrack opens the Pit Deck. Gallery shows the pit art. Stock Cred is credits and the disclaimer only.
