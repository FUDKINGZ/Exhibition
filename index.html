GUIDE & DISCLAIMER
HOW TO PLAYTOUCH + KEYBOARD
Touch: L and R walk. LP and RP punch. LK and RK kick. BK holds block. TH throws only while the ammo box is lit. Hold R with BK to push. The push keeps the block pose and shoves only while both are held.
Keyboard: S or the Left Arrow walks left. L, R, or the Right Arrow walks right. Hold either side to keep walking. D is LK. F is LP. G holds block. H throws, and only while ammo remains. J is RP. K is RK. Right plus Block is the push.
Moving left or right plays that fighter’s walk.gif. If the file is missing, the walk uses idle.gif. Stopping returns to idle. A push keeps the block gif.
Each meter is a depth bar. It starts at MID. LONG is the green side. SHORT is the red side. A combo pushes LONG. Alternate sides, or a punch and a kick, inside a short window. A lone hit, a same-side repeat, or a late hit does not build LONG. A miss or a blocked attack pushes SHORT and breaks the combo. A block stops the hit. It does not heal. There are no hit popups. The meter is the read.
A full LONG meter is a short squeeze. It chimes, flashes SQUEEZE!! across the casing, and arms 3 throws. The ammo count sits in the square box on the inner edge of each meter. A throw spends one. Close contact is 1, inside 32% of your width is 2, and farther is 3. Knockback is 8, 16, and 24. A blocked throw does no damage and no knockback. When the ammo is gone, the meter drops and has to be built again. Health is the dollar bar. A landed hit drops money. Power and throw ammo carry between rounds of the same match.
After the match, liquidate them. Walk left, walk left, then right punch, or let the count finish. The pin drops, the crash hits, and the money blows up off the floor. EXIT leaves for the title. SAVE, in Arcade, writes the run. CONT or NEXT leaves that screen.

The Left Arrow and the Right Arrow also walk. Attack keys fire once per press.
